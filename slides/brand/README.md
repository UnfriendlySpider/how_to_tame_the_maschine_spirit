# Brand files for the talk

`slides/deck.yaml` names which file goes where (`brand:`).

| File | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| `evidenze-logo.png` | the title slide and the closing slide, top right |
| `evidenze-germany-earth.png` | the title slide, bottom right |
| `phuse-connect-2026.png` | the title slide, top left |
| `eu-ai-black.png` | every generated image, top right, on the slides and in the paper's figures, and the line on the title slide that explains it |

The public copy of the repository leaves out the two Evidenze files and the
conference logo (the author's decision, 2026-11-15): they are their owners'
marks, and no permission to pass them on is recorded here. Without them the
deck builds with a placeholder in each place, and `--final` refuses.

**Source.** The two Evidenze files are the ones https://www.chrestos.de shows, accessed
2026-10-06: the page says that Chrestos is part of the Evidenze group and goes
by the name Evidenze Germany. `evidenze-logo.png` is its `evidenze_logo.png`
(1254 × 216 px, transparent background), the Evidenze logo with the three bars
of the E in green, blue and navy. `evidenze-germany-earth.png` is its
`evidenze_germany_earth.png` (1046 × 904 px), the earth the title slide of the
author's PHUSE EU Connect 2025 deck (ML04) carried, then under the Chrestos
logo.

**Colours.** The deck uses the colours evidenze.com defines in its stylesheet:
navy `#091A40` (`--bs-primary-dark`), blue `#1D4EBD` (`--bs-primary`) and green
`#27B564` (`--bs-success`), the colours of the logo's three bars. The site's
fonts, Museo Sans and Reckless Neue, do not ship with PowerPoint, so the deck
is set in Arial, as ML04 was.

If Evidenze's marketing has an official logo file or a slide template, put it
here and point `deck.yaml` at it. The public copy takes only this file and
the AI label from this folder, so a new logo stays out of it as well.

**AI label.** `eu-ai-black.png` is the file `LABEL_AI_black.png` from the
PNG set on the European Commission's page "EU icons for labelling
AI-generated content"
(https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-icons-labelling-ai-generated-content,
accessed 2026-10-09), unchanged: 2363 × 2364 px, "AI" in white in a black
circle on a transparent background. The build crops the transparent margin
on the slide, so the file stays as the EU published it. The icons are
Annex 1 of the Code of Practice on marking and labelling AI-generated
content, published on 10 June 2026. The set has three icons, each in black,
white, and both at 50 % transparency:

- **AI GENERATED**, for content "fully generated by AI with no human-created
  elements or human editorial control (apart from prompting)";
- **AI MODIFIED**, for human-made content partly modified with AI;
- **AI**, the basic icon, wherever AI was involved, and beside a text label
  or a second, interactive layer of one's own.

The deck uses the basic icon, in black for the black-and-white look: it is
small and keeps out of the drawings' way (the author's choice, 2026-10-09,
after a first build with AI GENERATED). The Code requires only the capitalised "AI"
as the icon's main element and encourages, without requiring, a word such as
"generated" in or next to it (Measure 1.1). The Commission's page adds that
in its user tests "performance improved across all measures when the basic
icon was accompanied by a text label". In a talk about AI, "AI" alone might
also read as the topic rather than the source of a picture. So the title
slide, where the icon first appears, sets it beside one line that says what
it marks (`ai_legend` in `deck.yaml`).

The page says the icons "are made publicly available for everyone to use
freely, without the need for attribution to the Commission or the AI
Office". It also says that their use is optional while the labelling duties
of Article 50 of the AI Act are not, and that use by someone who has not
signed the Code does not signal adherence to it. Placement follows the
Code's Measure 1.2: "in an appropriate place where no intervening overlay
elements exist (e.g., in the top right corner of an image or video deep
fake)".

The paper's figures carry the same file, 4 mm across, so that the letters
stay readable in print; the disclaimer says once what the icon marks.
`tools/template.typ` cannot measure the file, so `ai-label` there holds the
circle's place in it (1524 px across, from 372, 420). A new file needs those
numbers changed with it.

**Conference logo.** `phuse-connect-2026.png` is the conference's own logo,
the file `eu-connect-2026` on PHUSE's page for the event
(https://events.phuse.global/event/EUConnect26/summary, accessed
2026-10-15). It reads "phuse·connect, The Clinical Data Science Conference",
"EU Connect 2026", "SEC (Scottish Event Campus), Glasgow" and "15–18
November", in white and teal on a transparent background, 800 × 164 px. It
is made for the page's blue header and would vanish on the white title
slide. So the file sets it, unchanged, on that blue, `#003584`, the header
colour of the page's theme, with a margin of 28 px at the sides and 24 px
above and below (833 × 187 px). Some PHUSE EU Connect 2024 decks carry the
conference banner on their title slide in the same way.

PHUSE's events terms (V3, June 2024), which cover delegates and speakers,
say nothing about its logos. Its sponsorship and exhibition terms (V3, June
2023) say that a sponsor or exhibitor "may not use the PHUSE logos without
prior written permission from PHUSE" (clause 1.6). Evidenze is not among the
event's sponsors (seen 2026-10-15).

PHUSE's Code of Conduct, which the event page links (V5, April 2026, p. 4;
https://phuse.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/Communications/Forms/PHUSE+Code+of+Conduct.pdf,
accessed 2026-10-28), says in its part for all members of the community:
"You must never use PHUSE's (or its affiliated entities') logos, marks, or
other protected information or property for any business or commercial
venture without pre-clearance from the PHUSE Board of Directors." The
conference logo on this talk's own title slide serves no business venture,
so it stays. Keep `phuse-connect-2026.png` off posts: the LinkedIn teaser
(`../../promo/linkedin/README.md`) names the conference in text only, and for a post
use the official social media graphics PHUSE offers speakers in its Speaker
Resource Centre (read 2026-10-28: "our official social media graphics"). If
in doubt, ask PHUSE, or delete the `conference_logo` line in `deck.yaml` to
drop the logo.
